# Project Roadmap & Vision

## Project Vision
AlumniNet aims to be the leading platform for alumni-student networking, mentorship, and career development globally.

## Core Mission
Connect talented students with experienced alumni to facilitate:
- Career mentorship and guidance
- Job referral opportunities
- Professional networking
- Skill development
- Industry insights

## 2026 Goals
- Reach 500+ active alumni
- Engage 200+ students
- Enable 50+ mentorship pairs
- Facilitate 30+ job referrals
